# Key Talking Points

Use these points to keep the interview conversation focused.

## Lead With

- Messaging reliability is more than sending and consuming messages.
- DLQs, retries, idempotency, replay, and backpressure are design concerns.
- Producer safeguards make routing failures visible.

## Go Deeper On

- How duplicate messages influence consumer design.
- Why poison messages need a bounded failure path.
- When asynchronous messaging adds more complexity than value.

## Avoid Overclaiming

- Do not say queues automatically make systems reliable.
- Reliability comes from explicit failure handling and observability.
