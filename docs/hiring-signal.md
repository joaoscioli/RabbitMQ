# Hiring Signal

This repository is meant to support hiring conversations for roles that involve
asynchronous systems and backend integration.

## What It Proves

- Ability to reason about retries, DLQs, replay, and idempotency.
- Awareness of duplicate delivery, backpressure, and message-contract drift.
- Communication of failure behavior beyond the happy path.

## Role Fit

Best aligned with backend, integration, platform, and distributed-system roles
where reliable messaging matters.
