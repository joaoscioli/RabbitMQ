# Hiring Manager One-Pager

## Project Positioning

A messaging architecture repository focused on RabbitMQ, failure handling, and
operational reliability.

## Why It Is Relevant

Asynchronous systems need explicit contracts, retries, DLQs, idempotency, and
observability.

## What It Demonstrates

- Practical understanding of messaging trade-offs.
- Awareness of duplicate delivery and poison-message handling.
- Ability to discuss when messaging is worth the complexity.

## Interview Hook

Ask how this design handles a failed or duplicated message.
